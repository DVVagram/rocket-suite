What runs what

  • Design — OpenRocket 24.12 (GPL-3.0), loaded in-process through JPype.
  • Conversion — RocketSerializer (MIT), including its OpenRocket jar driver.
  • Flight — RocketPy (MIT); no physics is re-implemented in the suite.
  • ORHelper — used only through RocketSerializer's own driver; there is one JVM per session.
